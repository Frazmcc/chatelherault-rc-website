Weekly Gatherings
at the Park

Every weekend, Chatelherault Country Park transforms into a technical playground. Our community meets at the historic hunting lodge before descending into the river valley trails together.

Whether you pilot a 1/10 scale crawler or a custom-built technical rig, we welcome all enthusiasts to join our weekly trail runs. We focus on technical precision over speed, tackling the natural sandstone formations and challenging woodland climbs that define the Scottish landscape.
