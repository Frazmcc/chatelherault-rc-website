Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What We Collect

We only collect information needed to run the club website and admin features, including account details for approved admins/moderators and content submitted through website forms.

How We Use Data

Data is used for account login, moderation, and publishing club content such as media and members rigs. We do not sell your data.

Storage And Security

We use Cloudflare-hosted services and apply role-based access controls for admin areas. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal data through public forms.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the club through the contact page.
