Safety Guidelines

Basics for trail walking and RC crawling at Chatelherault.

  • Wear suitable footwear with good grip. The ground is frequently wet, muddy, and slippery.
  • Move carefully on inclines, roots, and uneven sections. Slipping on mud is a constant issue on the trails.
  • Use wooden bridges and steps where available. We are gradually adding more to improve access.
  • Keep a safe distance from steep edges, water, and unstable banks.
  • Do not block public paths. Give way to walkers, dogs, and other park users.
  • Keep vehicles at controlled speeds near people and in tight technical sections.
  • Children should remain supervised at all times during meets.
  • Bring weather-appropriate clothing and be prepared for rapidly changing ground conditions.
