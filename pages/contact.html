Common Technicalities

How do I join a run?

add

Check our 'Meetups' page for scheduled weekend events. New members are encouraged to attend a "Class 1" session first to get familiar with the group and terrain.

What equipment is mandatory?

add

At minimum, a 1/10 scale crawler, fully charged LiPo/NiMH battery, and a compatible transmitter. Waterproofing is highly recommended given the Scottish climate.

Are there membership fees?

add

Currently, we operate as a community collective. There are no mandatory fees, though we occasionally pool resources for trail maintenance or trophies.

Can I bring a basher?

add

Our primary focus is technical crawling and trailing. High-speed bashers are generally not suitable for our organized trail runs for safety and pace reasons.
